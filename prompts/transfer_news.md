## CRITICAL RULES

### 1. Transfer direction (FC Porto perspective)
- **Entrada** = player comes TO FC Porto (Porto is the buyer). "Clube de Origem" = selling club.
- **Saída** = player leaves FC Porto (Porto is the seller). "Clube de Destino" = buying club.
- Don't be fooled by article titles about other clubs — an article about "Santa Clara" may report a player moving TO Porto (Entrada), not FROM Porto (Saída).
- If unsure about direction, **omit the entry**.

### 2. Confirmed vs Rumor classification
**Entradas/Saídas (CONFIRMED only):**
- Officially announced by FC Porto (fcporto.pt), OR
- Reported as closed/completed by multiple Tier 1 sources, OR
- Confirmed by official sources (Liga Portugal, CMVM)
- Confirmation indicators: "oficial", "confirmado", "apresentado", "assinou", "negócio fechado", "acordo total", "medicalizado"

**Rumores (everything NOT confirmed):**
- Rumor indicators (Portuguese): "apontado a", "interesse de", "pode ser", "em negociações", "pretende", "sonda", "liga-se a", "rumoreja-se", "segundo fontes", **"perto de"**, **"a caminho de"**, **"ruma a"**, **"próximo de"**, **"deve rumar"**, **"poderá assinar"**, **"em vias de"**, **"a um passo de"**, **"negociação avançada"**, **"acordo em vista"**
- ⚠️ Phrases like "perto de rumar aos EUA", "a caminho do Chelsea", "deve assinar pelo" are **RUMORS**, not confirmations.
- **When in doubt → classify as rumor.** A player "on the exit list" without a confirmed destination = rumor.
- Examples:
  - ✅ "Hwang In-beom assinou pelo FC Porto" → Entradas
  - ✅ "Nilton Varela troca FC Porto por Estrela da Amadora (oficial)" → Saídas
  - ❌ "Diogo Costa pode ser vendido ao Chelsea" → Rumores de Saída
  - ❌ "FC Porto interessado em Marcos Leonardo" → Rumores de Entrada

## Rumor aging rules
Based on the publication date of the most recent source article:
1. **Fresh (≤ 2 weeks):** keep as-is, no marker
2. **Aging (2–4 weeks, no new developments):** prefix player name with `⏳` (e.g., `⏳ João Silva`)
3. **Stale (> 4 weeks, no new developments):** **REMOVE entirely** — no longer relevant
4. **Exception — reactivated:** if an old rumor has a new update (new article, new bid) within 2 weeks → treat as fresh, no `⏳`

Note: `⏳` = aging indicator, not missing data. Don't confuse with `❓`.
